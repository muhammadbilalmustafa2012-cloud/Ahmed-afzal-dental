Privacy Policy

At Azeem Afzal Dental Surgery Centre (located in Jinnah Colony, Faisalabad, Pakistan), we respect your medical privacy and are committed to protecting personal identifiable information that you share with us through appointment bookings and consultations.

1. Information We Collect

When requesting a dental consultation or treatment, we collect your name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment times, and any dental symptoms you describe.

2. How Your Information Is Handled

Your contact information is strictly utilized to coordinate your dental consultation, verify your appointment slots, and communicate post-treatment dental care instructions. We do not sell or rent patient data to external commercial third parties.

3. Clinical Inquiries

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices, you may reach our clinic directly at Imam Bargah Road near Ideal Bakery, Jinnah Colony, Faisalabad or phone +92 370 1309729.
